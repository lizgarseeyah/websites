About

One person, a few honest obsessions.

I'm a creator and builder who happens to be most at home with a club or racquet in hand. The sport taught me the discipline; the camera taught me to notice; the work taught me to ship. I keep them in the same place because, for me, they've always been the same instinct — pay attention, then make something true.

Off the court I architect software and advise teams on getting it right. On it, I'm chasing a cleaner swing and a sharper eye. Everything here is some version of that.
