About

Senior cloud & AI expertise, without the enterprise overhead.

Scriptech Consulting is a cloud, AI, and data practice for organizations that want to modernize deliberately. The work draws on years architecting cloud and Microsoft Power Platform solutions inside large, regulated environments: cloud strategy, identity, data pipelines, AI agents, dashboards, and the governance and reliability work that decides whether a system survives production.

Most teams don't struggle with cloud or AI because the technology is weak. They struggle because no one owns the architecture, the governance, or the adoption. Scriptech is that owner: embedded, accountable, and focused on making the build defensible and usable from day one.
