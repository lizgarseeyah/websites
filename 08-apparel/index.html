The story

Built on something that holds.

Liri Co. started with a simple conviction: the things we wear can carry what we believe. Sport taught discipline; faith gave it a reason. We put the two in the same place — apparel that performs on the course and the court, and means something when the round is over.

We design for the whole family because the values do too. The adult lines hold the standard; the kids and baby lines carry it forward through original characters of our own — no borrowed logos, just our world. Everything is made to last, made to move, and made to be worn with conviction.

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart."
