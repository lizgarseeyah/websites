About

We make models accountable, not just impressive.

Most AI demos collapse the moment a real policy is applied. Nyra starts from the opposite end: the rules, the record, the review. We wrap reasoning models in a governance layer that enforces what's allowed, captures why each step happened, and hands you an artifact a compliance team can actually sign off on.

The result is intelligence that behaves like infrastructure — predictable, observable, and built to be inspected. Teams in finance, health, and the public sector use Nyra to move fast without trading away the ability to explain themselves later.
