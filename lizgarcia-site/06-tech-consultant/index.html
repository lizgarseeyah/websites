About

Built by someone who has shipped this, not just slides.

Aegis is a specialist advisory practice for AI agent governance on the Microsoft Power Platform. The work comes from years architecting Copilot Studio deployments inside large, regulated organizations — environment strategy, DLP, identity, orchestration and the unglamorous reliability work that decides whether an agent survives contact with production.
